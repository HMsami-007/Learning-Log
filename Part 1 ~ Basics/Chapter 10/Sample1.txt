In a circular queue, we make use of the pointer of the front and rear of the queue.

Each time we add a new item to the queue, we add it to the new empty space and then we increment the rear pointer by one.
Each time we remove an item from the front of the queue we remove it and then increment the front pointer by one.
If any of the pointers are equal to the length of the array then we need to reset that pointer position to zero.
